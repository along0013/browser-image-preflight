# Browser Image Editing Review Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing a browser-based image editing workflow.

## Before editing

- [ ] The page explains whether the selected image is uploaded or processed locally.
- [ ] The input formats and expected download format are visible before a visitor starts.
- [ ] The visitor can see the selected image before making any destructive edit.
- [ ] The page tells the visitor to edit only images they own or are authorized to modify.

## During editing

- [ ] The editing area has a clear next action.
- [ ] A visitor can undo or start again without reloading the whole site.
- [ ] The workflow does not imply that a complex image repair will always be perfect.

## Before publishing a commercial recommendation

- [ ] The recommended product is relevant to the task a visitor has just completed.
- [ ] The page does not claim to provide products or content supplied by a third party.
- [ ] A referral or affiliate link has a plain-language disclosure beside it.
- [ ] The disclosure is readable at mobile width.
